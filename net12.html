Net12 — 12-fold cable net

Six line families — horizontal, vertical, ±30°, ±60° — form the base lattice. 1000 cables of equal length start at the beam, travel only along the lattice (any direction, zip-tied at turns) and end at randomly placed bulbs, spending their length where the net has the fewest cables so far.
